These are target allocations. We'll publish how support was actually spent once a year on this page.

Questions

Is my support tax-deductible?

No. Web.Life is an independent publication, not a registered charity, so contributions aren't tax-deductible.

How do I pay?

After you pledge, we email you a secure payment link from an established payment processor. You complete payment on the processor's page, not on Web.Life.

Can I cancel a monthly pledge?

Yes, at any time. Reply to any receipt or use the contact form.

Do supporters influence what you publish?

No. Supporters can vote on which free tool we build next, but editorial decisions stay independent. See our editorial standards.
