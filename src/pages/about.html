A website is a living thing. We help it thrive.

Web.Life is an independent publication for anyone who owns or runs a website: small businesses, creators, publishers, developers and families keeping a corner of the internet alive.

Why we exist

Most advice about websites focuses on one moment: how to build one, or how to rank one. But websites have whole lives. They're launched, they grow (or don't), they earn, they get attacked, and eventually they're sold, merged, handed on or switched off. Problems in one stage are usually caused by decisions in an earlier one. A domain registered to a forgotten email address becomes a crisis years later. A site built without records becomes impossible to sell.

Web.Life organises everything around those five stages, so you can see where you are, what comes next and what to put in place now.

What you'll find here

  • Free tools that run in your browser, from cost calculators to valuation estimators.
  • Guides written in plain English, each ending with practical next steps.
  • Web Pulse, a self-updating digest of industry headlines and new public data breaches.
  • Expert help for owners who'd rather have it done, through our services desk and talent network.

Editorial standards

  • Accuracy first. We check facts against primary sources such as official documentation from search engines, standards bodies and platforms, and we date every guide.
  • Approaches over brands. We explain how to choose, rather than ranking products for commission.
  • Clear labels. Sponsored placements and any affiliate links are labelled where they appear. Sponsors never see or approve our content.
  • Corrections. If something is wrong or out of date, tell us. We fix errors promptly and note significant changes.
  • Not professional advice. Our guides are general information. For legal, tax or financial decisions, consult a qualified professional.

How Web.Life is funded

We keep the site free with a mix of display advertising (served by Google AdSense), labelled sponsorships, optional reader support and paid expert services. Tools never require an account, and nothing you type into them is sent to us.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: readable contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus, text that resizes, reduced motion when you ask for it, and a dark theme. If anything gets in your way, let us know and we'll fix it.
