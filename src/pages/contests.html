Official rules

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. Supporting Web.Life does not improve your chances.

  1. Organiser. The contests are run by Web.Life.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or older. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Web.Life staff, judges and their immediate families may not enter.
  3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the first day to the last day of the calendar month shown on this page, ending at 23:59 in the entrant's local time zone as displayed by the countdown.
  4. How to enter. Submit the form above. One entry per person per contest. Entries must be your own work or submitted with the owner's permission.
  5. Content rules. Entries must be lawful, must not infringe anyone's rights, and must not contain adult, hateful, deceptive or malicious content. We may disqualify entries that break these rules.
  6. Judging. A panel chosen by Web.Life scores entries using the published criteria for that contest. Judges' decisions are final.
  7. Winners. Winners are notified by email and announced within 14 days after the contest closes. If a winner doesn't respond within 14 days, we may choose an alternate.
  8. Prizes. Prizes are as described on this page at the time of entry, are non-transferable and have no cash alternative. Sponsor prizes are provided by the sponsor, who is responsible for fulfilling them. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  9. Publicity. By entering, you allow Web.Life to display your name, website address and entry in connection with the contest.
  10. Privacy. Entry details are used only to run the contest, as described in our privacy policy.
  11. Changes. Web.Life may modify, suspend or cancel a contest if circumstances beyond its control affect fair operation.
