Earn stage

Earn: turn visits into dependable income.

Most profitable websites mix several revenue streams. The right mix depends on your audience's intent, not on what's fashionable.

Revenue follows intent. A reader comparing products is worth more to an affiliate partner; a reader with a problem they want solved is worth more as a lead; a loyal reader is worth more as a subscriber. Display advertising earns from all of them, but rarely the most from any.

A practical way to plan:

  1. Measure. Know your monthly sessions, pages per session and top pages before choosing a model.
  2. Match model to page. Guides suit display ads, comparisons suit affiliate links, problem pages suit lead forms, tools suit sponsorships.
  3. Stay policy-safe. Ad networks reward original content and clear layouts. Never encourage clicks or hide what is an ad.
  4. Track every channel. Tag campaign links so you can see which sources bring revenue, not just visits.
  5. Diversify. A site with three income streams is more resilient and, when you sell, more valuable.
