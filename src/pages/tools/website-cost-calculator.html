How to read your estimate

The calculator splits cost into two kinds. Recurring costs (hosting, apps, email, marketing and the domain renewal) repeat every month or year for as long as the site exists. One-off costs (a theme, design help and launch content) are paid once. Your first year includes both; from year two you mostly pay the recurring part.

The preset prices are typical starting points, not quotes. Hosting and builder plans usually advertise a lower first-term price and renew higher, so use the renewal price when you plan beyond year one.

Where people overspend

  • Apps they never use. A plugin or add-on for every idea adds up. Start with what launch day actually needs.
  • Premium design before content exists. A beautiful template with three thin pages won't attract visitors. Write first, polish later.
  • Paying for features twice. Many builders include forms, email marketing or analytics that people also buy separately.

Where people underspend

  • Content. Useful pages are what bring visitors, links and enquiries. Budget time or money for them.
  • Backups and security. Cheap insurance compared with rebuilding a hacked site.
  • A domain term longer than one year. It removes a renewal risk during your busiest months.
