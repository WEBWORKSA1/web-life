What structured data does


Structured data is a standard vocabulary (from schema.org) that describes a page in a way machines can read without guessing: this is a business, here is its address; this is an article, here is its author and date. Search engines use it to understand pages and, for some types, to show enhanced results. It doesn't guarantee a rich result, and it must describe content that's actually visible on the page.


Which type to use


    

  • Organization on your homepage: name, logo and official profiles.

  • Article on blog posts and guides.
