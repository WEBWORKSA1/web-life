The formula behind the estimate

Display ad income is pageviews ÷ 1,000 × RPM, where RPM is what you earn per thousand pageviews. Affiliate income is sessions × click-out rate × purchase rate × commission. Lead income is sessions × lead conversion rate × the value of a lead to you or to a buyer. Product and sponsorship income are entered directly. Everything is monthly.

Why your RPM will differ

Ad revenue per thousand pageviews varies with the audience's country, the topic, the season, the device mix and how many ads a page carries. Advertisers bid more in commercial topics and in the last quarter of the year. That's why the presets are only starting points: after a month of real data, replace them with the RPM your ad account reports.

Read the sensitivity table first

The table shows your total if one assumption is halved or raised by half. The row with the widest spread is the lever worth working on. For most new sites it's traffic; for established sites with steady traffic it's often lead value or affiliate conversion, both of which respond to better page design and clearer offers.

Ground rules

  • Never encourage or incentivise clicks on ads. It breaks ad-network policies and can close your account.
  • Disclose affiliate relationships clearly near the links.
  • Sell leads only with the visitor's consent to be contacted.
