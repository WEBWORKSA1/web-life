Naming rules that keep reports clean


    

  • Agree a short list of mediums and stick to it. Analytics tools group channels using the medium value, so inventing new ones scatters your data.
    • 

  • Use lowercase and underscores, never spaces.
    • 

  • Keep a shared spreadsheet of every tagged link you create.
    • 

  • Don't tag internal links between your own pages. It overwrites the original source of the visit.
    •
