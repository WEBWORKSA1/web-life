Protect stage

Protect: keep the site safe, online and yours.

The common disasters are boring: a reused password, a lapsed renewal, a backup nobody tested. That's good news, because boring problems have cheap fixes.

Security for a small website is mostly about access and recovery. If only the right people can log in, and you can restore the site from a recent backup within an hour, most incidents become an inconvenience instead of a catastrophe.

Put these in place, in this order:

  1. Lock the front doors. Unique passwords from a password manager and two-factor authentication on your registrar, host, email and CMS.
  2. Auto-renew everything. Domain, hosting and certificates, billed to a card that won't expire next month.
  3. Back up off-site. Automatic daily backups stored somewhere other than your host, and a restore you have actually practised.
  4. Update on schedule. Core software, themes and plugins. Remove anything you don't use.
  5. Monitor. An uptime check every few minutes and an alert that reaches you, not an inbox nobody reads.

Generate strong credentials, translate uptime promises into real minutes, and keep an eye on newly published breaches below.
