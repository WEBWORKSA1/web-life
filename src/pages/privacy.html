Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026. This policy explains what information Web.Life collects, why, who else is involved, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Our tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us.
  • We receive information only when you choose to send a form (for example, a project brief, newsletter signup or contest entry).
  • We show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure those ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Information you give us

When you submit a form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, website address and message, plus the page you sent it from and the time. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-processing service, which processes the data to deliver it to us. We use this information only to respond, provide what you asked for (a review, a quote, a payment link for a pledge, contest administration, the newsletter) and keep records of our correspondence.

Advertising and cookies

Web.Life uses Google AdSense to display advertising. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice, we ask Google to show non-personalised ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also see a consent message managed through Google's consent tools, where you can make more detailed choices.

Storage in your browser

We use your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember a few settings on your device: your colour theme, your cookie choice, the website stage you last selected, your health-check ticks and your digital legacy planner entries. This information stays on your device; we can't see it. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Embedded and connected services

  • YouTube videos load only when you press play, through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). After you press play, Google's privacy policy applies to the video.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • Web Pulse loads live community stories from the Hacker News search API in your browser, and links to original publishers and to Have I Been Pwned.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations.

Why we process information

We process form data to respond to your request or perform an agreement you ask for, and with your consent where you tick a consent box. Advertising cookies are used on the basis of consent where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence and project records for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, generally no more than three years. Career applications are kept for up to 12 months. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form with the topic "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Web.Life is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we don't knowingly collect their information. Contests are open only to people aged 18 or older.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Forms are sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send passwords or payment card numbers through any form.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on the homepage for 30 days.
