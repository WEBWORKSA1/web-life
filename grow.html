Grow stage

Grow: get found, load fast, keep people reading.

Traffic grows when search engines can crawl your pages, understand them, and trust that visitors will be glad they clicked.

Growth is mostly compounding small things done consistently. The sites that win in search are rarely the cleverest; they are the ones that answer questions clearly, load quickly on a phone, and keep publishing.

The order that tends to move the needle fastest:

  1. Indexing. Verify the site in Google Search Console, submit a sitemap and fix anything listed as "not indexed" that should be.
  2. Titles and descriptions. Each page gets a unique title that matches what people search for, and a description that earns the click.
  3. Speed. Compress and correctly size images, defer scripts you don't need, and watch your Largest Contentful Paint.
  4. Content depth. Update your best pages with what readers still ask about. Merge thin pages into stronger ones.
  5. Internal links. Link new pages from older, already-ranking ones so they are discovered and understood faster.

Use the meta checker before publishing, the page-weight budget before adding another script, and the schema generator to help search engines read your page correctly.
