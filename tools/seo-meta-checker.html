Why pixels, not just characters

Search results truncate titles and descriptions by display width, not by a fixed character count. Wide letters like W and M use more room than i and l, so two 60-character titles can behave differently. The preview measures your text in the font search results typically use and cuts it where a desktop result usually would. Treat the limits as guides: search engines may rewrite snippets when they think another part of your page answers the query better.

Writing a title that earns the click

  • Put the words people search for near the start.
  • Say what the page delivers: a calculator, a checklist, a comparison, an answer.
  • Keep every title unique across your site.
  • Add your brand at the end only if there's room.

What the audit checks

The audit reads the HTML you paste and checks twelve basics: title, meta description, a single H1, image alt text, canonical URL, mobile viewport, language attribute, indexability, Open Graph tags, structured data, content length and links. It can't see what JavaScript adds after the page loads, and it doesn't fetch anything from the web. For how search engines crawl, index and rank pages, read SEO fundamentals for new websites.
