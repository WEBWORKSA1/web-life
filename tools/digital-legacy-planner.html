Why this matters for website owners

Domains and hosting are rented, not owned outright. If renewals stop because the card on file expires or the owner can't act, a domain can lapse and be registered by someone else, taking the website and its email with it. A short inventory, kept somewhere a trusted person can find it, prevents that.

What to record

  • Domains: the registrar, the expiry date and the account email.
  • Websites: the host, the platform and where backups are stored.
  • Email: which provider hosts the mail for each domain.
  • Money: payment processors, ad accounts and affiliate programmes that pay out.
  • The keys: the password manager that holds everything, and how your contact can access it in an emergency.

Use the built-in legacy features

Many major platforms now let you nominate a legacy or inactive-account contact. Set them up, then note in this plan which accounts have one. Review the plan once a year, around the time you renew your most important domain.
