What robots.txt can and can't do


A robots.txt file asks well-behaved crawlers not to fetch certain paths. It is a request, not a lock: it doesn't hide pages from people, and a blocked URL can still appear in search results if other sites link to it. To keep a page out of search results, let it be crawled and add a noindex robots meta tag instead. To protect private content, require a login.


The AI crawler options


Some companies publish named crawlers that collect content for AI training, and say they honour robots.txt. Blocking them is a business decision about how your content is reused; it doesn't affect normal search crawling. Google-Extended controls use in Google's AI models without affecting Google Search.


Sitemap tips


    

  • List only canonical, indexable pages you want found: no redirects, no noindex pages.
    • 

  • Keep each sitemap under 50,000 URLs and 50 MB uncompressed.
    • 

  • Upload both files to your site's root and submit the sitemap in Search Console.
