What this estimate is, and isn't

The calculator adds the time to transfer your page's bytes at the chosen speed, a latency cost for the round trips needed to fetch your requests, and a small allowance for processing JavaScript. Real loading is more complex: browsers download in parallel, cache files between visits and can show content before everything arrives. Use the number to compare options and set a budget, then confirm with a real test in PageSpeed Insights.

Why 2.5 seconds

Google's Core Web Vitals treat a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less as good. If simply downloading your page takes longer than that on a typical mobile connection, the main content can't appear in time.

Trim in this order

  1. Images. Serve modern formats (WebP or AVIF), size them to the space they fill, and lazy-load anything below the fold.
  2. JavaScript. Remove scripts you don't use, defer the rest, and question every third-party widget.
  3. Fonts. Use one or two families, subset them, and preload the one your headline needs.
  4. Requests. Combine small files and drop decorative extras.
