Launch stage

Launch: from idea to a live, findable site.

The decisions you make in the first month (name, platform, structure) are the ones that are hardest to change later. Make them on purpose.

Launching is less about design and more about foundations. A website that starts with a clear purpose, a domain you control, a platform you can maintain and a handful of genuinely useful pages will outgrow a prettier site built on guesswork.

Work through the stage in this order:

  1. Purpose. Write one sentence that says who the site is for and what they can do on it. Every page should serve that sentence.
  2. Name and domain. Short, easy to say, easy to spell, and checked against existing trademarks. Register it in an account protected by two-factor authentication.
  3. Platform. Pick a builder, CMS or static site based on who will update it and how often, not on which demo looked best.
  4. Core pages. Home, About, Contact, a privacy policy and your first three to five pages of real content.
  5. Launch checks. HTTPS, mobile layout, page titles, analytics and Search Console verified before you announce anything.
