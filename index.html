Questions people ask

Is Web.Life free to use?

Yes. Every guide and all fourteen tools are free and need no account. The site is supported by advertising, sponsorships, optional donations and paid expert services.

Do the tools store what I type?

No. The tools run entirely in your browser. Only the forms you choose to submit, such as a project brief, are sent to us.

Who handles project requests?

The Web.Life team reviews every brief and replies within one business day with next steps, a scope question or a referral to a vetted specialist.

How do you choose what to recommend?

We recommend approaches, not brands, and we say when something depends on your situation. Sponsored placements are always labelled. Read our editorial standards.
